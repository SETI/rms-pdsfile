# plans/

This directory holds planning documents for the rms-pdsfile modernization
effort. The **active plan** is `2026-07-25-modernization-plan.md` — the
complete, self-contained specification the effort executes (v2; PR-01–PR-09
of v1 are merged and recorded there as completed). The mini-holdings /
hermetic-test-data concept was removed from the effort on 2026-07-25; its
design record and future options live in `2026-07-25-mini-holdings-plan.md`
(status: deferred), supported by the measured analysis in
`critiques/2026-07-21-unified-mini-holdings-analysis.md`. Superseded plans
and completed per-PR sub-plans are kept in `archive/` (currently the v1 plan
and the PR-03/04 and PR-08 sub-plans). Deviations from the active plan are
recorded as dated addendum files here and acknowledged by the owner before
the deviating change merges.
